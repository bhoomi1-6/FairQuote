TITLE: Pay-As-You-Go / Telematics Policies

How it works:
- Insurance cost based on real driving data
- Black box or app tracks acceleration, braking, cornering, mileage

Best for:
- Students or young drivers
- Low-mileage drivers (<6,000 miles per year)
- Safe, defensive drivers who want lower costs

Avoid if:
- You drive aggressively or often at night
- Privacy concerns with continuous tracking

Pros:
- Potentially cheapest option for careful drivers
- Fair pricing based on actual usage

Cons:
- Penalties for bad driving
- Limited mileage plans
