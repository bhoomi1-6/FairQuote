TITLE: UK Car Insurance Risk & Pricing Factors

Vehicle-based factors:
- Age of vehicle
- Engine size
- Value
- Fuel type (EV vs petrol/diesel)
- Theft risk rating
- Safety features

Driver-based factors:
- Age
- Licence years
- Claims history
- Occupation
- No-claims bonus

Location-based factors:
- Crime rate
- Traffic density
- Accident risk
- Rural vs. city

Usage-based factors:
- Mileage
- Business/private use
- Parking location (road vs. driveway vs. garage)
