# Young Driver, New Driver and Overseas Licence Clauses

**Insurer:** FairQuote Insurance Limited
**Applies to:** All policy types
**Section Reference:** DRIVER-COND-01

## Young Drivers (Aged 17–24)

Drivers aged 17 to 24 are classed as young drivers and are subject to the following conditions:

- A higher compulsory excess applies to young drivers, shown separately on the policy schedule, in addition to the standard compulsory and voluntary excess.
- Young drivers may be required to have a telematics ("black box") device fitted as a condition of cover, which monitors driving style including speed, braking, and time of day driven.
- Cover for young drivers on high-performance vehicles (engines over 2.0 litres, or a manufacturer power output exceeding 200bhp) is not available under a standard policy and requires specialist underwriting referral.

## New Drivers (Held a Full Licence for Under 12 Months)

A driver is classed as a new driver for the first 12 months after passing their practical driving test, regardless of age. In addition to any young driver conditions that may also apply:

- New drivers must declare all provisional and full licence penalty points, including those still on a provisional licence, at the time of purchase.
- The No Claims Discount Protection option described in the Comprehensive and Third Party, Fire and Theft terms is not available to new drivers until they have completed 12 consecutive months without a fault claim.

## Overseas and International Licence Holders

FairQuote Insurance will provide cover to drivers holding a valid licence issued outside the United Kingdom, subject to the following:

- **EU/EEA full licences**: Treated equivalently to a UK full licence, provided the licence remains valid and has not been suspended or revoked in its country of issue.
- **Non-EU/EEA full licences**: Accepted for up to 12 months from the date the driver became a UK resident. After 12 months of UK residency, the driver must hold a valid UK provisional or full licence for cover to continue.
- **International Driving Permits (IDP)**: Accepted only when held alongside a valid overseas licence recognised by the DVLA, and only for drivers who have been UK residents for less than 12 months.

Drivers must declare their licence type, country of issue, and date they became a UK resident (if applicable) at the time of purchase. Failure to accurately declare this information may result in a claim being reduced or declined.

## Named Young or New Drivers on an Existing Policy

Adding a young or new driver as a named driver on an existing policyholder's vehicle (rather than as the main policyholder) will still increase the premium to reflect the additional risk, and the young/new driver excess and any telematics condition apply equally to named drivers as to policyholders.
