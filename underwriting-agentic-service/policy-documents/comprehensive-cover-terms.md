# Comprehensive Cover — Policy Terms

**Insurer:** FairQuote Insurance Limited
**Policy Type:** Comprehensive Motor Insurance
**Section Reference:** COMP-01

## What Is Covered

Under Comprehensive cover, FairQuote Insurance will pay for:

1. **Accidental damage** to your vehicle, including damage caused by your own driving error, regardless of fault.
2. **Fire damage**, including damage caused by an electrical fault, arson, or accidental fire.
3. **Theft or attempted theft** of your vehicle, including damage caused during a break-in or theft attempt.
4. **Malicious damage** caused by a third party, including vandalism.
5. **Windscreen and glass damage**, subject to the windscreen excess set out in your policy schedule.
6. **Third-party liability**, covering injury to other people and damage to their property, up to the limits set out in your Certificate of Motor Insurance.
7. **Personal accident cover**, providing a fixed benefit for death or permanent disability of the policyholder resulting directly from a motor accident, up to £10,000.
8. **Medical expenses**, up to £150 per injured person following an accident involving your vehicle.
9. **Courtesy car**, provided while your vehicle is being repaired at an approved repairer following an accident covered under this policy, subject to availability.

## New-for-Old Replacement

If your vehicle is less than 12 months old from first registration and is declared a total loss due to an insured event, we will replace it with a new vehicle of the same make, model, and specification, subject to availability. Vehicles older than 12 months are settled on a market value basis as described in the Claims Procedure section.

## Excess

A standard compulsory excess applies to every claim, shown on your policy schedule. Additional voluntary excess, if selected at the time of purchase, is added to the compulsory excess. Excess amounts do not apply to third-party-only injury claims made against you by another party.

## Geographical Limits

Comprehensive cover applies within the United Kingdom, the Channel Islands, and the Isle of Man. Cover extends automatically to any European Union member state for up to 90 days per trip. Cover outside these territories requires prior written agreement from FairQuote Insurance.

## No Claims Discount Protection

Policyholders who have held five or more consecutive years of no-claims discount may purchase No Claims Discount Protection. This allows up to two claims within a policy year without loss of no-claims discount at renewal, although the premium may still increase to reflect claims experience.
