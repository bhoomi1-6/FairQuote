# Commuting versus Social Use — Definitions

**Insurer:** FairQuote Insurance Limited
**Applies to:** All policy types
**Section Reference:** USE-DEF-01

The "Car Usage" declared on your policy schedule determines what type of driving is covered. Using your vehicle for a purpose beyond what is declared may result in a claim being reduced or rejected. FairQuote Insurance recognises three usage classes.

## Social, Domestic and Pleasure (SDP)

This is the most restrictive and lowest-risk usage class. It covers:

- Driving for personal, leisure, and family purposes.
- Shopping trips, visiting friends or family, and holidays.
- Driving to and from a place of full-time education.

It does **not** cover:

- Driving to a fixed place of work on a regular basis.
- Any use in connection with a business or trade.

## Social, Domestic, Pleasure and Commuting (SDP+C)

This usage class includes everything covered under Social, Domestic and Pleasure, plus:

- Driving directly between your home and a single, permanent place of work.
- Driving between your home and a train station, bus stop, or car park used as part of your regular commute.

It does **not** cover:

- Driving between multiple business sites in the course of your work (this requires Business Use).
- Using the vehicle to carry out the duties of your job, such as visiting clients or making deliveries.

Commuting cover assumes a single, fixed commute. Policyholders who regularly travel to more than one workplace, or who use the vehicle for work-related driving beyond the commute itself, must declare Business Use instead.

## Business Use

This usage class includes everything covered under Social, Domestic, Pleasure and Commuting, plus:

- Driving between multiple places of work.
- Driving as part of your job, such as visiting clients, making deliveries, or attending meetings at various sites.

Business Use does **not** automatically include:

- Hire or reward driving, such as operating as a taxi, courier, or ride-share driver, which requires a separate specialist policy.
- Use by a business for a fleet of vehicles, which is arranged under a commercial fleet policy.

## Why This Matters for Your Premium

Usage class is one of the factors used to calculate your risk-based premium, because time spent driving during rush hour or on unfamiliar routes for work increases the statistical likelihood of a claim. Declaring the correct usage class ensures your premium accurately reflects your actual driving pattern and that any claim you make is valid.

## Changing Your Declared Usage

If your circumstances change — for example, you start a new job requiring a regular commute, or your employer requires you to drive between sites — you must inform FairQuote Insurance as soon as possible. Failing to update your usage class may invalidate future claims.
