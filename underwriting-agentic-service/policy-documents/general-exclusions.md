# General Exclusions

**Insurer:** FairQuote Insurance Limited
**Applies to:** All policy types (Comprehensive and Third Party, Fire and Theft)
**Section Reference:** GEN-EXCL-01

This policy does not cover any claim arising directly or indirectly from the following circumstances, regardless of the level of cover purchased.

## 1. Unlicensed or Disqualified Driving

No cover applies if, at the time of the incident, the driver:

- Did not hold a valid UK or exchangeable overseas driving licence for the category of vehicle being driven, or
- Was disqualified from driving, or
- Had their licence revoked or suspended.

This exclusion applies even if the policyholder was unaware that the driver was unlicensed or disqualified.

## 2. Racing, Rallying and Track Use

No cover applies while the vehicle is being used for:

- Racing, rallying, or speed trials of any kind.
- Use on a racetrack, circuit, or closed course, including "track day" events, even if driven at moderate speed.
- Off-road driving not on a recognised public road, except in an emergency to avoid an accident.

## 3. Driving Under the Influence

No cover applies if the driver was:

- Over the legal alcohol limit for driving in the country where the incident occurred, or
- Under the influence of drugs not prescribed by a registered medical practitioner, or
- Refused to provide a specimen of breath, blood, or urine when lawfully required to do so.

## 4. Wear, Tear and Mechanical Failure

This policy does not cover the cost of repairing or replacing parts that fail due to:

- Normal wear and tear.
- Gradual deterioration, rust, or corrosion.
- Mechanical, electrical, or electronic breakdown not caused by an insured event such as fire, theft, or accident.

Damage that results **from** a mechanical failure — for example, an accident caused by sudden brake failure — may still be covered under the accidental damage or third-party sections, subject to the terms of your policy.

## 5. Using the Vehicle Outside Its Permitted Use

No cover applies if the vehicle is used for a purpose not declared on the policy schedule, including:

- Using a vehicle insured for "social, domestic and pleasure" for business or commuting purposes.
- Using the vehicle for hire or reward (for example, as an unlicensed taxi or courier) unless specifically declared and agreed.
- Carrying passengers or goods for payment where not permitted by the policy.

## 6. Deliberate Acts

No cover applies for loss or damage caused deliberately by the policyholder or any person insured to drive under this policy.

## 7. War, Terrorism and Confiscation

No cover applies for loss or damage caused by war, invasion, act of a foreign enemy, civil war, rebellion, terrorism, or confiscation of the vehicle by any government or public authority.
