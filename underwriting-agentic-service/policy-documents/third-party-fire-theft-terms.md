# Third Party, Fire and Theft — Policy Terms

**Insurer:** FairQuote Insurance Limited
**Policy Type:** Third Party, Fire and Theft (TPFT)
**Section Reference:** TPFT-01

## What Is Covered

Third Party, Fire and Theft cover provides a reduced level of protection compared to Comprehensive cover. Under this policy, FairQuote Insurance will pay for:

1. **Third-party liability**, covering injury to other people and damage to their property, up to the limits set out in your Certificate of Motor Insurance. This is the legal minimum required to drive in the UK.
2. **Fire damage** to your own vehicle, including damage caused by an electrical fault or accidental fire.
3. **Theft or attempted theft** of your own vehicle, including reasonable damage caused during a break-in or theft attempt.

## What Is Not Covered Under TPFT

Unlike Comprehensive cover, this policy does **not** pay for:

- Accidental damage to your own vehicle caused by a collision, regardless of fault.
- Windscreen or glass damage, unless it results directly from a covered fire or theft event.
- Vandalism or malicious damage that does not involve fire or theft.
- Courtesy car provision.

Policyholders who want their own accidental damage covered should consider upgrading to a Comprehensive policy.

## Excess

The compulsory excess for fire and theft claims is shown on your policy schedule. No excess applies to third-party injury or property damage claims made against you.

## Total Loss Settlement

If your vehicle is declared a total loss due to fire or theft, FairQuote Insurance will pay the market value of the vehicle immediately before the loss, as determined by an independent trade guide. New-for-old replacement is not available under Third Party, Fire and Theft cover.

## Geographical Limits

Cover applies within the United Kingdom, the Channel Islands, and the Isle of Man, with automatic extension to EU member states for up to 90 days per trip, consistent with Comprehensive cover.
